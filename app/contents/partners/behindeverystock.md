## What Is BehindEveryStock?

[BehindEveryStock](https://behindeverystock.com/) is an all-in-one **stock research platform** covering more than 70,000 stocks worldwide. It combines real-time insider data, congressional trade tracking, AI-driven sentiment analysis, valuation models, and fundamental financial metrics into a single, intuitive dashboard.

Instead of jumping between half a dozen websites and spreadsheets, investors can find everything they need in one place — from a company's P/E ratio to what members of Congress are buying and selling.

## Track Insider Trades in Real Time

One of the most powerful features of BehindEveryStock is its [insider trading tracker](https://behindeverystock.com/). Corporate insiders — CEOs, CFOs, board members — are required to disclose their stock transactions. These filings can signal confidence or concern about a company's future.

BehindEveryStock aggregates these disclosures in real time, presenting them as colour-coded timelines that make patterns instantly visible. You can see at a glance whether insiders have been buying heavily or offloading shares over the past twelve months — a data point that often precedes major price moves.

## Follow Congress and Senate Stock Trades

Under the STOCK Act, members of the U.S. Congress must disclose their stock transactions. These [congress stock trades](https://behindeverystock.com/) are public record, but finding and interpreting them is tedious without the right tools.

BehindEveryStock tracks quarterly congressional portfolio positioning and breaks it down by party, chamber, and individual legislator. Whether you want to see what the Senate Finance Committee chair is buying or how congressional portfolios shifted after a major policy announcement, the data is there — organised and searchable.

## AI-Powered Sentiment Analysis

Market sentiment drives short-term price action as much as fundamentals do. BehindEveryStock uses [AI stock sentiment analysis](https://behindeverystock.com/) to scan news articles, analyst reports, and social media chatter, then plots sentiment over time alongside the stock price.

This gives investors a visual correlation between how the market *feels* about a stock and how the price actually moves. Divergences between sentiment and price can signal opportunity — a stock the market is pessimistic about that keeps climbing, or one with euphoric sentiment that may be overvalued.

## Fair Value Models and Valuation Metrics

Is a stock overvalued or undervalued? That is the question every investor asks, and BehindEveryStock provides a proprietary [fair value calculator](https://behindeverystock.com/) to help answer it.

The platform generates fair value estimates based on multiple valuation methodologies, then compares them to the current market price. Combined with distribution charts for key ratios — P/E, P/S, P/B — versus sector peers, investors get a clear picture of where a stock sits relative to its industry.

This is the kind of analysis that institutional funds pay thousands for. BehindEveryStock makes it accessible to individual investors at a fraction of the cost.

## Analyst Price Targets and Financial Data

Professional analysts publish price targets and buy/hold/sell recommendations that can move stocks significantly. BehindEveryStock compiles these into a clear distribution view showing the high, low, and median targets, plus a timeline of how [analyst recommendations](https://behindeverystock.com/) have shifted over time.

On the fundamentals side, the platform provides five-year trends for revenue, assets, cash flow, and other key financial metrics. Proprietary radar charts score each stock across Growth, Value, Dividend, and Risk on a 1–10 scale, with an automated SWOT analysis that highlights strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats at a glance.
