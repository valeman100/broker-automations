## An Online Gallery Without Borders with ArtLab

Getting your work seen has always been the hardest part of being an artist. Physical galleries are gated by geography, budget, and who you happen to know — and for most artists, the door never opens at all. Talent has never been the bottleneck. Access has.

That is the problem [ArtLab](https://www.art-lab.space/) was built to solve. It is an independent **online gallery without borders**: always open, free for everyone, and built so any artist can put their work in front of an audience within minutes.

## What Is ArtLab?

[ArtLab](https://www.art-lab.space/) is a virtual gallery and portfolio platform for contemporary art across every medium — painting, sculpture, photography, digital work. Artists create a free account, upload their work, and go live without a credit card or a curator's approval.

Visitors browse the salon like they would a real gallery: room by room, work by work, with artist interviews that give the pieces context instead of leaving them as thumbnails on a grid. The platform's own words say it best — "Art has always belonged to everyone, but not everyone has been made to feel welcome."

## Free to Start, Curated When You Are Ready

ArtLab's [collaboration model](https://www.art-lab.space/collaborate) scales with how much support an artist wants:

- **Open** — free. One work, presented on your own terms.
- **Space** — €10/month. Your own site inside the gallery, up to 10 works, self-directed.
- **Curated** — €299 setup plus €50/month. Your space is built and arranged by the ArtLab team, with curator-led strategy and a monthly one-hour call reviewing your work.

The Curated tier is representation, not hosting: the curator becomes your interface — filtering requests, advising on direction, and representing you externally. As they put it, "the work becomes ours."

## Artists Keep Their Work

Copyright and sales rights stay entirely with the artist. ArtLab takes permission to promote work on its platform and channels — nothing more. No ownership claims, no licensing to third parties, no fine print that quietly signs your catalogue away.
