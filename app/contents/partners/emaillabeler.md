## An Inbox That Sorts Itself with Email Labeler

Email is the one queue nobody ever finishes. Two hundred unread messages, and the three that actually matter are buried between a shipping notification and a 50%-off promo. Folders and filter rules were supposed to fix this, but rules only catch what you predicted — and every new sender means another rule to write.

That is the problem [Email Labeler](https://www.email-labeler.com/) was built to solve. It is an **AI email management tool** that reads, understands, and labels every message in your inbox, so the sorting happens before you ever open it.

## What Is Email Labeler?

[Email Labeler](https://www.email-labeler.com/) connects to your mailbox and applies your own labels automatically. Instead of writing brittle rules based on sender addresses and subject-line keywords, you describe in plain words what belongs where — "invoices and bank statements", "anything from a client about a policy" — and the AI decides.

The result is the same inbox, reorganised: Shopping, Work, Finance, Promo, Travel, Personal. Nothing is deleted, nothing is moved out of reach. The mail is simply labelled, so scanning it takes seconds instead of minutes.

## Describe Your Labels, Skip the Rules

This is the part that makes the difference. Traditional filters match on strings; Email Labeler matches on meaning. A supplier who changes email address, a client writing from their phone, a receipt that never says "receipt" anywhere — all of it still lands in the right place, because the model reads the content rather than pattern-matching the headers.

You define the categories and their descriptions once. From then on the classification adapts to new senders on its own.

## Three Steps and It Runs Itself

Setup is deliberately short: connect your account over secure IMAP, configure your labels, and let the AI take it from there. It works with Gmail, takes about two minutes, and needs no installation, no plugin, and no credit card to start.

After that it is a background process. Mail arrives, it gets labelled, and you get an inbox that is already sorted when you open it.

## Privacy-First by Design

Handing an AI access to your mail is a real decision, and Email Labeler treats it that way:

- **Encrypted storage** — credentials are encrypted at rest.
- **Emails never stored** — content is processed in memory, then discarded.
- **Read-only access** — it never deletes, sends, or modifies your mail.
- **Revoke anytime** — access can be removed from your Google settings.

The processing is transient by design: the system needs to read a message to classify it, and it keeps nothing afterwards.
